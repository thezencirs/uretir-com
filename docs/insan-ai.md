# InsanAI

## Purpose

InsanAI connects scientists, engineers, inventors, researchers, entrepreneurs, and technology leaders to Uretir's manufacturing knowledge graph.

It is a source-aware profile system, not a biography generator.

## Profile contract

Every profile supports:

- original biographical summary;
- occupations and affiliations;
- major contributions;
- important ideas;
- books, talks, and research;
- influence;
- related technologies, companies, institutions, articles, and guides;
- official and primary sources;
- subject and source reviewers;
- review and next-review dates.

## Publication gate

A profile cannot be published or emit Person structured data without:

- at least two independent reliable sources;
- at least one primary, university, or official source;
- an attributable contribution;
- subject-matter and source reviewers;
- completed review and next-review dates.

The initial profile collection is intentionally empty. AI agents must not create plausible names, biographies, affiliations, awards, books, talks, or research records to populate the interface.

## Originality

Profiles summarize facts in Uretir's own editorial language. Source material is used for verification and attribution, not copied into a composite article.
